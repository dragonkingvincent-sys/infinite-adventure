Privacy Policy — Infinite Adventure (无限冒险)

Developer: Happy Playing  |  Effective Date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Happy Playing ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the mobile application Infinite Adventure (无限冒险).

1. Information We Collect

You can play Infinite Adventure in Guest Mode without providing any personal information. If you choose to sign in or use online features, we may process the following limited data:

  • Account Information (Optional): If you sign in with Google or create a custom username, we receive your display name, email address (for Google Sign-In), and a unique user identifier (UID) via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Gameplay & Leaderboard Data: Your unlocked classes, achievements, stage clear progress, and challenge mode scores are stored locally on your device (localStorage) and synced to Google Cloud Firestore when logged in so you can use cloud saves and global leaderboards.
  • No Sensitive Personal Data: We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or payment card details, and we do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain core gameplay, cloud save synchronization, and global leaderboard rankings.
  • To identify your account across devices when you choose to sign in.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to provide login, cloud save, and leaderboard functionality. Google's use of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Local game data can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. If you have logged in and wish to request deletion of your cloud save and leaderboard record, please contact us via the email below with your account name or Google Sign-In email, and we will delete your data promptly.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data deletion requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer (Happy Playing) via Google Play developer contact email.

隐私权政策 —《无限冒险》

开发者：Happy Playing  |  生效日期：2026年11月23日

本隐私权政策说明 Happy Playing（以下简称“我们”）在您使用《无限冒险》（Infinite Adventure）应用时如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

您可以在“游客模式”下直接游玩本游戏，无需提供任何个人信息。如果您主动选择登录账号或使用联机排行榜功能，我们仅会处理以下必要数据：

  • 账号信息（可选）：当您使用 Google 账号一键登录或注册自定义游戏账号时，我们会通过 Google Firebase Authentication 获取您的显示名称、电子邮箱（仅限 Google 登录）及唯一用户标识（UID）。
  • 游戏进度与排行榜数据：您的已解锁职业、成就、怪物图鉴、关卡进度及挑战纪录会保存在您的设备本地（localStorage），登录账号后将同步至 Google Cloud Firestore，用于提供云存档和全服联机排行榜功能。
  • 不收集敏感信息：本应用不会收集您的精准定位、通讯录、相册、相机、麦克风或支付信息，也未接入任何第三方广告追踪 SDK。

2. 信息的使用方式

  • 用于提供游戏基础功能、跨设备云存档同步及全服排行榜展示。
  • 我们承诺绝不向任何第三方出售、出租或交易您的个人数据。

3. 第三方服务

本应用使用 Google Firebase（身份验证与 Cloud Firestore 数据库）提供登录与联机排行榜服务，相关数据处理遵循 Google 隐私权政策。

4. 数据保存与删除

您随时可以通过清除应用数据或卸载应用来删除本地存档。如果您希望删除已同步至云端的游戏存档与排行榜记录，请通过 Google Play 商店页面提供的开发者联系邮箱与我们联系，并提供您的游戏账号名称，我们将在核实后尽快为您删除。

5. 隐私权政策的更新

我们可能会适时更新本隐私权政策。更新后的内容将直接发布在本页面上。
